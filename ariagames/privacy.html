Privacy policy

Your data stays on your watch

Applies to Kart Watch GP, StarAce and ARIAgb. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The games have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Progress, settings and high scores are saved locally on your Apple Watch and never leave it.

Local multiplayer

Versus races connect watches directly to each other over your local network. No game servers are involved, nothing is sent to us, and no personal information is exchanged beyond in-race gameplay data, which is discarded when the race ends.

Third parties

The games do not include third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

Children

The games collect no data from anyone, including children.
