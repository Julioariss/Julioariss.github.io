Support

Need a hand?

Controls

Turn the Digital Crown to steer or move through menus. Swipe or tap to act, and hold the screen centre for 3 seconds to open the pause menu in games that support it.

Installing

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find the game under Available Apps and tap Install. Games run on the watch itself.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases and refunds are managed by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Still stuck

Write to orbmailapp@gmail.com with the game name, your watch model and watchOS version, and what happened.
