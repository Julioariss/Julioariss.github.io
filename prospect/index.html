Prospect

Find who to sell to. Talk to them today.

Describe what you sell and where. Prospect sweeps the whole area on Apple Maps, finds real businesses that could buy from you, ranks them by demand with Apple Intelligence, and drafts your first message. Then it becomes your CRM: call, WhatsApp, email, routes, pipeline and follow-ups — in one place.

How it works

  • Campaign. Tell it what you sell and your ideal client. Pick a country, a city and a radius.
  • Sweep. An adaptive grid search over Apple Maps discovers businesses, deduplicates them and reads their public websites for contact data.
  • Score. Apple Intelligence rates demand 0–100, explains why now and writes an opener. Works fully without it, too.
  • Act. Call, WhatsApp, email, get directions, save to Contacts. Every touch is logged.
  • Follow up. Kanban pipeline, next actions with due dates, a daily Today inbox, analytics.
  • Autonomous radar (Pro). Once a day, iOS wakes Prospect to explore a new slice of your area — never the same one twice — and alerts you to hot leads.

Privacy by architecture

Your pipeline never leaves your device. No servers, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Data is encrypted at rest with a key stored in the Secure Enclave-backed keychain. The only network calls are to Apple Maps and to the public website of a prospect you discovered.
