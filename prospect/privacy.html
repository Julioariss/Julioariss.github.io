Privacy Policy

Prospect is designed so that we cannot see your data. This policy explains what the app does and does not do.

Data we collect

None. Prospect has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data. The app contains no third-party SDKs and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your company profile, campaigns, prospects, notes and activity history are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, encrypted with AES-GCM. The encryption key lives in the iOS Keychain and travels only inside your own encrypted backups and device-to-device migration. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Network access

  • Apple Maps (MapKit) to search for businesses in the area you choose. Apple's privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • Public websites of prospects you discovered, fetched over HTTPS only and without cookies, to read publicly listed contact details.
  • App Store (StoreKit) to process an optional subscription. Apple handles payment; we never see card details.

On-device intelligence

Scoring and message drafting use Apple Intelligence's on-device model (Foundation Models). Prompts and results never leave your device. When Apple Intelligence is unavailable, Prospect uses a deterministic local engine.

Permissions

  • Location (while using) — optional, only to center a campaign on where you are.
  • Contacts — optional, only when you tap “Save to Contacts”.
  • Notifications — optional, to tell you when the autonomous radar finds new hot leads.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — optional, to lock the app.

Third parties and prospects' data

Prospect surfaces information that businesses publish themselves (name, address, phone, website). You are responsible for contacting them in accordance with applicable laws in your country.

Children

Prospect is a business tool not directed to children under 13.

Resumen en español

Prospect no recopila ningún dato. Todo se guarda cifrado en tu dispositivo; no hay cuentas, servidores ni analítica. Solo se conecta a Apple Maps, al sitio web público de los prospectos que descubres y a App Store para la suscripción opcional. La inteligencia corre en tu iPhone o iPad.
