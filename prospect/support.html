Getting started

  1. Fill in My company: what you sell, in one clear sentence. This powers every search and message.
  2. Create a campaign: describe your ideal client, pick country, city and radius, generate the search plan.
  3. Tap Sweep the area. Results appear in Radar, on the Map and in the Pipeline.

Try it without setting anything up

On the welcome screen tap Explore with sample data. Everything shown is fictional and nothing is saved. Leave it from Settings.

Apple Intelligence

Requires a compatible device (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iPad with M1 or later) with Apple Intelligence enabled in iOS Settings. Without it, Prospect still works fully with its built-in engine.

Autonomous radar

Part of Prospect Pro. iOS decides the exact time based on your usage, usually while charging. It explores a new slice of your area each day and never repeats one; the weekly deep pass only runs while plugged in.

Backups

Settings → Export encrypted backup creates a file protected with a passphrase only you know. Import it on another device from Settings. Your data is also included in your iCloud/iTunes encrypted device backups.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases in Prospect on a new device.
