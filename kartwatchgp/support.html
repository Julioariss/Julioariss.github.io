Kart Watch GP — Support

Kart Watch GP is an Apple Watch kart-racing game by Julio Aris Serey.

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to julio.aris.s@gmail.com and you will get an answer within a few days.

Tips: turn the Digital Crown or swipe to steer, tap to use items, hold a turn to drift and release for a mini-turbo, tap in the air to pull a trick.
