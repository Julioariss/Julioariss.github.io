Kart Watch GP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Kart Watch GP is an Apple Watch game made by Julio Aris Serey.

We do not collect any personal data. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

  • Your progress (unlocked cups, medals and best times) is stored only on your watch and in your iCloud/watch backups managed by Apple. It never leaves your device.
  • "VS Race" lets nearby watches race each other over your local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Only race positions are exchanged directly between the watches during the race; nothing is sent to any server.
  • No data is shared with third parties.
  • The game is suitable for all ages and contains no in-app purchases.

If you have questions, write to julio.aris.s@gmail.com.
