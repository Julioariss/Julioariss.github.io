StarAce — Support

StarAce is an Apple Watch arcade space shooter by Julio Aris Serey.

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to julio.aris.s@gmail.com and you will get an answer within a few days.

Tips: swipe to move the ship, use the roll buttons to deflect fire, hold the screen centre for 3 seconds to pause, and save your bombs for the bosses.
